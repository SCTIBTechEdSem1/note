Routine Student Note

IT Project Management

Unit One: Introduction of Project Management [2 hours]
1.1 Overview of IT project management principles, methodologies, and best practices.
1.2 Role and responsibilities of an IT project manager.
1.3 Key success factors and challenges in IT project management.

Chapter 2: Project Initiation and Planning (8 hours)
2.1 Project Selection and Initiation (1 hour)
i. Importance of project selection and initiation
ii. Criteria and methods for project selection
iii. Project charters, feasibility studies, and business cases

2.2 Stakeholder Identification and Analysis (2 hours)
i. Understanding stakeholders and their roles in projects
ii. Techniques for identifying stakeholders
iii. Stakeholder mapping and analysis
iv. Assessing stakeholder needs, interests, and influence
v. Managing stakeholder expectations

2.3 Defining Project Objectives and Scope (2 hours)
i. Defining clear and measurable project objectives
ii. Establishing project scope boundaries and deliverables
iii. Developing a scope statement
iv. Scope validation and control

2.4 Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) Development (2 hours)
i. Introduction to Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
ii. Decomposing project deliverables into work packages
iii. Organizing and structuring the WBS
iv. Hierarchical numbering and work package descriptions

2.5 Project Scheduling Techniques (1 hour)
i. Overview of project scheduling
ii. Gantt charts and their applications
iii. Network diagrams (PERT and CPM)
iv. Critical path analysis
v. Task duration estimation and dependency identification
vi. Creating a project schedule

Unit Three: Project Execution and Control (10 hours)
3.1 Team Formation and Management in IT Projects (2 hours)
i. Importance of effective team formation and management in IT projects
ii. Team roles, responsibilities, and dynamics
iii. Team building and motivation techniques
iv. Conflict resolution and communication within project teams

3.2 Project Communication and Reporting (2 hours)
i. Importance of communication in IT projects
ii. Communication planning and stakeholder engagement
iii. Effective communication channels and tools
iv. Project reporting and status updates
v. Managing project documentation and knowledge sharing

3.3 Risk Management in IT Projects (2 hours)
i. Understanding project risks and their impacts
ii. Risk identification, assessment, and prioritization techniques
iii. Developing risk mitigation and contingency plans
iv. Monitoring and controlling project risks
v. Communication and stakeholder involvement in risk management

3.4 Change Management and Configuration Management (2 hours)
i. Change management processes and procedures.
ii. Change request evaluation and approval
iii. Change control boards and change log management
iv. Configuration management and version control
v. Impact of changes on project scope, schedule, and resources

3.5 Quality Assurance and Control in IT Projects (2 hours)
i. Importance of quality assurance and control in IT projects
ii. Defining quality standards and metrics
iii. Quality planning and quality control activities
iv. Testing and quality assurance techniques
v. Continuous improvement and lessons learned

Unit Four: Project Closure and Lessons Learned (4 hours)
4.1 Project Closure Activities and Deliverables (1 hour)
i. Importance of proper project closure
ii. Key activities and tasks involved in project closure
iii. Deliverables and artifacts to be produced during project closure
iv. Finalizing project documentation and obtaining sign-off

4.2 Project Documentation and Knowledge Transfer (1 hour)
i. Documentation requirements throughout the project lifecycle
ii. Organizing and archiving project documentation
iii. Knowledge transfer strategies and techniques
iv. Capturing lessons learned and best practices

4.3 Post-Project Evaluation and Lessons Learned (2 hours)
i. Importance of post-project evaluation
ii. Methods for evaluating project success and performance
iii. Analyzing project outcomes against objectives and metrics
iv. Conducting lessons learned sessions and capturing insights
v. Implementing improvements based on lessons learned
